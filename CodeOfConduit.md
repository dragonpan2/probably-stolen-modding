# Probably Stolen: Modding Code of Conduct

Modding is a privilege extended to the community in good faith. We ask all mod authors to respect the following rules.

## Respect players

- Do not include malicious code of any kind in your mod
- Do not collect, transmit, or store any player data
- Do not display misleading information or impersonate the base game
- Do not intentionally cause crashes, freezes, or performance degradation

## Respect other creators

- Do not redistribute another mod author's work without their explicit permission
- Credit any code, assets, or ideas you borrow from others
- Do not create mods that are designed to break or conflict with another mod intentionally

## Keep content appropriate

- Mods containing adult content must be clearly labeled as such in the mod description
- Do not include content that is hateful, discriminatory, or targeted at any individual or group

## Be transparent

- Accurately describe what your mod does in `manifest.xml` and any accompanying documentation
- Clearly disclose if your mod modifies saves, game economy, or other systems that affect the player's experience
